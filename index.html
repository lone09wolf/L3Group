Before we begin

A few useful answers.

Do I need finished drawings to enquire?

No. Tell us what you want to build or change, where it is, and what information you already have.

Do you work beyond the Western Cape?

L3 Group is based in the Western Cape and can consider suitable projects elsewhere by arrangement.

Can I ask about consulting only?

Yes. Select Construction Consulting in the enquiry form and describe the decision or stage where you need support.
